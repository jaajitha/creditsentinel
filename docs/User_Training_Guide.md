# User Training Guide

## 1. Getting Started (5 min read)
Welcome to the Credit Sentinel Approval Dashboard! This tool gives you a bird's-eye view of all loan applications and the decisions made by our analysts. 

**How to log in:**
Currently, you do not need a password to access the dashboard. You are automatically authenticated based on your secure access link, and your analyst profile is loaded seamlessly in the background.

**Dashboard Overview:**
When you open the dashboard, you will see:
* **Summary Cards:** At the top, providing quick counts of Total Applications, Approved, Rejected, and Under Review.
* **Charts:** Visual graphs showing weekly trends, status distribution, risk scores, and system speed (latency).
* **Alerts:** Colored warning boxes that appear automatically if approval rates drop, rejections spike, or the system is running slowly.

## 2. Filtering Applications
You can narrow down the data you see using several easy-to-use filters. When you apply a filter, the summary cards, charts, and application list will instantly update.

* **By Applicant Name:** Type a name into the search box to find a specific person.
* **By Analyst:** Use the Analyst dropdown (e.g., select "Divya" or "Guru Prasad") to see only the applications reviewed by that specific team member. 
* **By Decision/Status:** Click directly on the pieces of the "Decision Status Distribution" pie chart to filter by Approved, Rejected, or Under Review.
* **By Date:** Use the "From" and "To" date selectors to view applications from a specific time period.

## 3. Drill-down Workflow
If you want to see exactly *why* a decision was made, you can "drill down" into the history of any application.

1. Find the application you want to inspect in the list.
2. Click on the application row.
3. **View Decisions:** A detailed view will open showing the complete audit trail.
4. **Check Audit Trail:** You will see the specific decision (Approve/Reject/Review), the exact date and time, the analyst who made the call, and their personal notes explaining the reasoning.

## 4. Exporting Reports
You can easily save the dashboard data to share with your team or management.

* **Export PNG (Images):** Click the blue "Export PNG" button. This will take a snapshot of your charts and download it as an image file.
* **Export CSV (Spreadsheet):** Click the green "Export CSV" button to download a spreadsheet of the currently filtered applications.
* **Timing Expectations:** Exports are generated directly in your browser. They usually complete instantly (under 1 second), but if you have thousands of records, it might take a few seconds.

## 5. Common FAQs

**Q: My filters aren't working, and the dashboard is blank. What should I do?**
A: You might have selected a combination of filters that don't match any applications (e.g., searching for a name that doesn't exist within a specific date range). Clear your search box and set all dropdowns back to "All" to reset the view.

**Q: Why does the export take longer sometimes?**
A: The export speed depends on your computer's processing power and the amount of data you are downloading. If you are downloading a PDF of the entire year's history, it may take a few moments.

**Q: Who do I contact if the dashboard is broken?**
A: If the dashboard fails to load or shows a persistent red error message, please contact the IT Support team at support@creditsentinel.com or message the #frontend-support Slack channel.
